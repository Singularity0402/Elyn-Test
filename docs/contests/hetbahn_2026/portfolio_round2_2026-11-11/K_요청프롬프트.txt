Use case: photorealistic advertising / imagined future workplace and meal service.
Portrait 4:5, one human-scale future workplace scene. The idea: a prepared rice meal is quick to prepare, but workers have time to actually eat because work is handed over during their meal. Not a productivity chart, not food surveillance, not a medical or legal guarantee.
A contemporary customer-support office at twilight. In the lower foreground, an adult worker in simple office clothes, framed below the neck with no identifiable face, is calmly eating a proper meal: a clearly recognizable ordinary white ridged single-serving instant rice cup with a restrained diagonal red accent, soft appetizing opaque cooked short-grain white rice, two modest vegetable/protein side dishes, chopsticks gently lifting a normal bite. The hands and elbows rest naturally, no rushed posture, no spills. Food is on a small CLEAN DINING SURFACE distinct from keyboard/electronic equipment, with enough space between hot rice and electronics. No microphone near the food.
An ordinary work headset rests on its stand to the right, clearly put aside. In the midground RIGHT, a single digital status monitor sits facing almost square-on to the viewer, its empty flat dark navy display large and clean enough to add two Korean status lines later. A subtle small green round indicator suggests handoff completion but no generated characters or numbers. Behind it a receding organized support office is softly out of focus, a few anonymous co-workers seen only from behind continue working. The setting must feel like a real workplace handing tasks over, not an abandoned office, not a lone person secretly eating at their desk. No AI robot replacing the worker, no dramatic giant screen.
Composition: worker hands/rice and meal occupy lower third, status screen on right middle at roughly x=690–1020, y=480–720 in a 1080x1350 layout. Keep TOP LEFT 30% smooth dark navy wall/soft shadow with no objects to allow large Korean headline and official logo later. Calm warm window/desk practical lighting on food, deep blue office background, humane and hopeful everyday future, premium editorial food photography, visually coherent perspective. Dark clean bottom strip for later notice.
NO text, logos, labels, clocks, personally identifying records, customer data or watermarks anywhere. This is an imagined opt-in employer/meal-service partnership. Do not show buying rice as a requirement for a basic meal break or imply the rice package itself magically forces employers to grant rights.
